ABOUT SOFTWARE ENGINEERING

What Is Software
Engineering?

Software engineering is a structured and systematic approach to
designing, developing, testing, and maintaining software. It combines
programming, problem-solving, and project management to create
reliable, efficient, and user-friendly solutions for real-world needs.

Why Is It Important?

Software engineering powers the tools
and systems we use every day, such as
websites, mobile apps, online banking,
healthcare systems, and more. It helps
solve real problems, improves efficiency,
and connects people around the world.

What Do Software Engineers Do?

Software engineers design, write, test,
and maintain software. They turn ideas
into working applications,fix issues,
improve performance, and make sure
the software meets user needs.

Software Engineering vs. Programming

Programming is writing the code that
makes a program work. Software engineering
is broader—it includes planning, designing,
testing, and managing the entire software
development process.

Real-World Applications

Software engineering is used in many industries, including:

Healthcare

Electronic health records,
patient apps, and monitoring
systems.

Education

Learning management
systems, online classes,
and student tools.

Banking & Finance

Secure online banking,
mobile payments, and
fraud detection.

Business & Retail

E-commerce, inventory
systems, and customer
management.

Entertainment

Video games, streaming
platforms, and multimedia
applications.
